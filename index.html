Core Features

Built for the field, not the office

Photo-Based Detection

Fine-tuned EfficientNet-B4 on 180,000+ labeled rice disease images. 93% accuracy across 15 diseases. Simple traffic-light confidence display.

Voice Symptom Reporting

Call a local-rate number, describe symptoms in Vietnamese or Khmer. Whisper ASR transcribes, NLP extracts symptoms, returns diagnosis by voice or SMS.

Treatment Action Cards

Visual infographics showing: what to buy, how much to apply, safety precautions, and organic/IPM alternatives. Designed for low-literacy users.

Follow-Up Tracking

Automated check-ins at 3, 7, 14 days. "Has the yellowing stopped? Send a new photo." Escalates to expert if recovery stalls.

Human Expert Escalation

Confidence below 75%? High economic impact disease? Case routes to a verified agronomist. Target response: <4 hours during growing season.

Outbreak Heatmap Dashboard

Real-time map of disease reports for extension officers. Filter by disease, severity, commune. Early warning alerts when density exceeds thresholds.

Weather-Integrated Alerts

Proactive alerts: "High humidity forecast. Rice blast risk is HIGH. Inspect your paddies." Seasonal advisories based on disease-weather correlations.

Community Knowledge Feed

Anonymized success stories from nearby farmers. Builds social proof and trust. Moderated by cooperative leaders to prevent misinformation.

Human Impact

Lan's Story

Before SowSmart

Nguyễn Thị Lan, 52, wakes at 5:00 AM in Phú Tân commune. She walks to her 0.8-hectare paddy and sees brown, brittle leaves on three rows near the canal.

She asks her neighbor — he says "leaf burn." She rides her motorbike 7km to the pesticide shop. The owner sells her a broad-spectrum fungicide for 120,000 VND — a week's food budget.

She sprays the entire field without gloves. A week later, spots have spread. It wasn't rice blast — it was bacterial leaf blight, requiring completely different treatment.

The extension officer visits on day 12. By then, 30% of her crop is damaged.

-240 kg
Lost harvest — worth $96, nearly 10% of annual income

She also develops a rash on her forearms from pesticide exposure.

Next Season
After SowSmart

Same farm. Brown spots again — this time on five rows near the northern edge.

Lan opens Zalo, sends two photos and a voice note: "Lá bị nâu, bắt đầu từ đầu lá, mấy hàng gần mương."

Forty seconds later, she gets a diagnosis: Bacterial Leaf Blight, 87% confidence. The action card tells her to drain the paddy for 3–5 days and apply Bismerthiazol 20WP.

Total cost: 38,000 VND ($1.52) — less than a third of what she spent last season on the wrong chemical.

Day 3: SowSmart follows up. She sends a new photo. AI confirms improvement.

+1.1 tonnes/ha
Yield increase — worth $440 in additional revenue

Lan tells three women in her cooperative. By the next season, 47 farmers in her commune are using SowSmart.

She still has the rash scars from last year. She doesn't plan to get new ones.

3 Alternate AI-for-Good Concepts

Different problems. Same philosophy: AI that assists the underserved, not replaces the expert.

Public Health

MedBridge

AI Triage for Rural Community Health Workers in Bangladesh

Problem: 13,000 CHWs serving 65 million rural residents with only 6 weeks of training. Misreferrals waste money; missed referrals cost lives.

Solution: WhatsApp-based AI triage that guides CHWs through symptom assessment, suggests triage level, and generates referral notes in Bangla.

Key differentiator: Trained on Bangladesh-specific disease prevalence. Integrates with DHIS2. Every "emergency" recommendation requires CHW confirmation.

WhatsApp Bangla Offline SMS DHIS2
Labor Rights

HaqBot

Multilingual Labor Rights for Migrant Workers in the Gulf

Problem: 35 million South/SE Asian migrants in the Gulf face wage theft, contract violations, and unsafe conditions. They can't afford lawyers and don't speak Arabic.

Solution: WhatsApp chatbot answering labor rights questions in 8 languages, helping document violations, generating complaint letters, and connecting to free legal aid.

Key differentiator: Fine-tuned on Gulf labor laws (UAE, Saudi, Qatar). Human lawyers review all complaint filings before submission.

8 Languages Legal AI Gulf States WhatsApp
Climate Resilience

FloodSense

Community Flood Early Warning for Manila's Urban Slums

Problem: 4 million informal settlers in flood-prone areas. Government warnings are city-wide and arrive too late. Communities need hyper-local, real-time flood risk info.

Solution: Low-cost IoT water sensors ($15 each) + AI that predicts flood timing at the barangay level. Alerts via SMS and WhatsApp to community "flood captains."

Key differentiator: Hyper-local predictions, not city-wide. Community-owned sensors. 10 years of historical PAGASA flood data.

IoT Filipino SMS Alerts Community-owned
